Heavy Cleaning & Special Conditions

Additional charges may apply when significantly more labor is required due to:

  • Heavy dirt, grease, dust or bathroom buildup
  • Excessive pet hair or significant appliance/baseboard buildup
  • Large amounts of remaining contents or excessive trash
  • Pest/live infestation conditions, hoarding conditions, or unusual odors
  • Additional cleaning requested after the original walkthrough

Trash & Junk Removal

Standard cleaning pricing does not include full-property trash, furniture, estate contents, appliances, construction debris, or bulk junk removal. Normal cleaning-related trash and small amounts of debris are included. Larger removal needs must be discussed and quoted separately.

Specialty Cleaning

The following are not automatically included and require approval and/or separate pricing:

  • Biohazard or bodily-fluid cleaning; mold remediation
  • Pest/infestation or hoarding cleanup
  • Construction debris; paint, glue, caulk or adhesive removal
  • Carpet extraction/shampooing or specialty floor restoration
  • Large-scale junk removal or exterior pressure washing

Important Pricing Information

All listed prices are starting prices.

Final pricing is based on:

  • Property size and number of bedrooms/bathrooms
  • Overall condition and amount of remaining contents
  • Dust, dirt, buildup and appliance condition
  • Basement, garage and additional spaces
  • Number of windows when window cleaning is requested
  • Level of detail, add-on services and estimated labor required

A final quote may be provided after viewing the property or receiving sufficient information/photos to determine the scope of work.

Repeat Estate Sale Services

SwiftSweep Cleaning is happy to work with estate sale professionals on an ongoing basis. Our goal is consistent expectations, straightforward communication, dependable scheduling and predictable starting prices, while still accounting for properties that require significantly more work.

Properties can be evaluated individually so the appropriate level of cleaning can be selected based on the condition of the home and what is needed for that particular estate sale.

Our Goal

Whether the property needs cleaning before an estate sale or a final reset after the sale is complete, SwiftSweep Cleaning's goal is to leave the home clean, presentable and ready for its next step.
